About the department

Department of Mechatronics Engineering, RUET

RUET was the first public university in Bangladesh to introduce Mechatronics Engineering, launching the department in 2013 within the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering. The program blends mechanical, electronic, and computer engineering to train engineers for robotics, automation, and intelligent-systems work.

This alumni site is run by and for graduates of the department, a place to stay in touch, find your batchmates, and support current students the way alumni before you may have supported you.
